# Engine notes: Crazy Eights

Seats default 3 (support 2–4). Deal per docs/RULES_DECISIONS.md, turn up a starter card (if it is an 8, bury it in the stock and turn another — standard). Moves {type:'play', card, suit?} (suit REQUIRED when playing an 8: the named suit; otherwise must match the top card's suit — or the named suit after an 8 — or its rank), {type:'draw'} (one card; allowed any time the stock is non-empty; after drawing the same player continues), {type:'pass'} only when the stock is empty and nothing is playable. If both stock is empty and the discard pile has more than one card, you may choose to reshuffle the discard pile (except the top card) into a new stock using rngState — standard house rule; document your choice clearly and keep games terminating. First to empty their hand wins; betting: winner takes the pot — learner wins → +(players−1) units, else −1. If the game blocks (no one can play, stock empty), lowest card-point total wins (8 = 50, faces 10, aces 1, others pip) — document. Bots: easy plays the first legal card (8s whenever); normal saves 8s for when stuck, prefers matching rank to switch to its strongest suit, names the suit it holds most, plays high-penalty cards early, draws only when needed. Flags: perfect when winning without drawing a single card; luckyLastCard when the learner's final card was an 8; comeback when the learner had ≥ 3 more cards than the leader at some point and won; closeFinish when the runner-up had 1 card left. Content: order 30.
