# Engine notes: Andar Bahar

Seats: 0 = learner, 1 = dealer. Phase 'bet': learner move {type:'bet', side:'andar'|'bahar'} (the joker card is already face-up from setup so the learner bets after seeing it). Then the dealer's forced {type:'deal'} moves deal one card at a time alternately starting with Andar until a card of the joker's rank appears. Payout: Andar win +0.9 units, Bahar win +1, loss −1 (maxLossUnits 1). Coach explains that it's pure chance, why Andar pays slightly less (it gets the first card, so it wins a bit more often — compute and mention the approx probabilities from simulation, around 51.5% vs 48.5% for single-deck starting on Andar), and that neither side is a 'better' bet. Bot difficulty: the dealer has no choices; 'normal' and 'easy' learner bots (for simulations) pick a side (normal: Bahar for slightly better expected value; easy: random). Flags: luckyLastCard when the match came after 25+ cards (the long nail-biter) or on the very first card; closeFinish when the match came within the first 3 cards; bigPot never (single unit). Tags: 'first-card-match'. Simulation: also check the empirical Andar win rate over ≥ 20,000 deals is within a reasonable band of the theoretical value (compute it exactly or by large simulation in the test). Content: order 50, hook about the fastest card game at every Indian wedding/mela (keep it non-gambling: pretend coins).

## Board notes (UI)

Centre: the joker card big and glowing. Two long lanes: ANDAR (inside, top/left) and BAHAR (outside, bottom/right) like a real board printed on felt, each with its payout ('pays 0.9 to 1' / 'pays 1 to 1') and the learner's bet chip on the chosen lane. Bet phase: two big buttons 'Bet on Andar' / 'Bet on Bahar' (test ids ab-andar, ab-bahar). Dealing: the dealer persona deals cards alternately into the lanes as overlapping rows (they wrap on mobile), a running count 'Card 7', and when a matching rank lands it zooms/glows with 'MATCH!'. Keep it fast and exciting.
