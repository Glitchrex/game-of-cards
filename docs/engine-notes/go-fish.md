# Engine notes: Go Fish

Seats default 3 (support 2–5). Moves {type:'ask', target: seat, rank} — only ranks you hold, only other seats that still have cards. Resolution inside applyMove: target gives all cards of that rank (you go again) OR 'Go Fish': draw the top stock card (if any); if it is the asked rank you go again, otherwise turn passes. Completed books (4 of a rank) are laid down immediately (also after receiving cards / drawing / dealing). If a player's hand becomes empty on their turn: draw one card if the stock has any, else they're out and the turn passes. When the stock is empty and a player has no cards they are skipped. Game ends when all 13 books are made. Most books wins; ties share (k tied winners incl. learner → net (players−k)/k); learner wins → +(players−1) units (sole), loss −1. Bots: easy asks for a random held rank from a random opponent; normal remembers PUBLIC information (who asked for which rank, who received/gave which ranks, books made) to target likely holders, and prefers ranks it holds more of. Never read other hands. describeMove must not reveal what a bot drew. Flags: comeback when the learner trailed by ≥ 3 books at some point and won; closeFinish when the margin is 1 book; luckyLastCard when the learner's final book came from a 'Go Fish' draw of the asked rank; perfect when the learner gets ≥ 7 books. Content: order 20 — it's the gentlest first game for kids and families.

## Board notes (UI)

Opponent seats (avatar, card count, their books as small stacks). Centre: the 'pond' (stock) as a scattered pile with a count. Learner hand grouped by rank (so it's obvious which ranks you can ask for). Ask flow: pick a rank (tap a rank group in your hand or a rank chip row; test ids gofish-rank-<R>) + pick a player (tap a seat; test ids gofish-target-<seat>) → 'Ask' button (test id gofish-ask) with a sentence preview 'Ask Ravi for 7s'. Big friendly 'GO FISH!' splash when told to fish; books fly to your book area. Kid-friendly and joyful.
