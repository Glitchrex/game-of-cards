# Engine notes: War

2 seats (learner 0 vs bot 1). The learner's single move each round is {type:'flip'} (both players flip — one battle resolved per move, including any war chain: each war places 3 face-down + 1 face-up per player; a player with fewer than 4 cards uses their last card as the face-up card; a player with 0 cards at a war loses). To give the bot a seat, model the bot's flip as a forced move too OR keep currentPlayer = 0 always — choose whatever keeps the UI simple (document it); simulations will drive whichever seat is current. State keeps both piles (top = index 0), the last battle's revealed cards (for the UI), battle count, and history for flags. Won cards go to the bottom of the winner's pile in a fixed deterministic order (winner's cards first, then loser's, in play order). End: one player has all 52 cards, OR after 60 battles the player with more cards wins (equal = push) — per docs/RULES_DECISIONS.md. Net ±1 unit. Bot difficulty is irrelevant to War (no decisions) — still implement botMove (always flip). coach(): explain that War is pure luck, what a war is, and what just happened; suggestion = flip. Flags: comeback when the learner had ≤ 16 cards at some point and won; closeFinish when the final margin ≤ 4 cards (at the 60-battle cap); luckyLastCard when the last battle was a war won by the learner; bigPot never; tags 'war-won' count.
