# Engine notes: Spades

4 seats; partnerships 0 & 2 (learner + bot partner) vs 1 & 3. Dealer from seed; bidding starts left of the dealer: {type:'bid', tricks: 0..13} (0 = Nil). Then play: left of the dealer leads first; {type:'play', card}; follow suit if able; spades are trump; spades cannot be LED until broken (a spade was played on a non-spade lead) unless the leader holds only spades. Scoring per docs/RULES_DECISIONS.md: team contract = sum of non-nil bids; made → 10×contract + overtricks (bags); failed → −10×contract; nil bids score ±100 for that player's team, and the nil bidder's tricks do NOT count toward the partner's contract (standard) but do count as bags for the team (document your choice). Learner team wins if its hand score > opponents' (tie = push), ±1 unit. Bots: easy bids roughly (count aces/kings + spades), normal uses a decent bidding heuristic (sure tricks: A, K with length, spade length, voids) and nil only with very weak hands; play: win tricks needed for contract, avoid bags when contract made, protect partner's nil, cover partner. Flags: perfect when the learner's team makes its bid exactly or a nil succeeds (tag 'nil'); closeFinish if the score difference ≤ 10; comeback if the learner's team was behind on tricks needed with ≤ 4 tricks left and still made it; bigPot when winning by ≥ 100. Content: order 110. Teach partnership, bidding, nil, trump, breaking spades, bags.
