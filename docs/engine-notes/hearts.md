# Engine notes: Hearts

4 seats, one hand per game. Phase 'pass': each seat in turn order makes {type:'pass', cards:[3 codes]} (seat 0 first; passing left: seat i → seat (i+1)%4); after all four passed, cards are exchanged simultaneously (a player must not see what they receive before passing — bots choose from their own pre-pass hand). Then the holder of 2♣ leads it (legalMoves returns only the 2♣). Moves {type:'play', card}. Rules: follow suit if able; on the FIRST trick no point cards (hearts or Q♠) unless you have nothing else (if only hearts, a heart is allowed; standard: Q♠ only if you have nothing but hearts and Q♠); hearts cannot be LED until a heart has been played (broken) unless the leader has only hearts; Q♠ does not break hearts (standard modern rule — document). Trick winner = highest card of the led suit, leads next. Scoring: 1 per heart, 13 for Q♠; shooting the moon (one player takes all 26) → that player 0, others 26. Winner(s) = lowest hand score; betting per docs/RULES_DECISIONS.md: sole winner +3 units, k tied winners +(4−k)/k each, losers −1 (from the learner's view). Bots: easy follows suit with random/low cards; normal: passes high hearts/Q♠/A♠/K♠ and voids short suits, ducks under the current winner, dumps Q♠/high hearts when void, avoids taking Q♠, leads low, tracks played cards (public), simple shoot-the-moon defence.
