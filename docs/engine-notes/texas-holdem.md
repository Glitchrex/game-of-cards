# Engine notes: Texas Hold'em Poker

Seats default 4 (support 2–6), 100 chips each (option), blinds 1/2, button position from the seed. Streets: preflop, flop (3), turn, river, showdown. Deal the board as forced dealer steps? Simpler and acceptable: board cards are revealed automatically inside applyMove when a betting round closes (the UI animates the newly revealed board cards). Moves: {type:'fold'}, {type:'check'}, {type:'call'}, {type:'bet', amount}, {type:'raise', to}, {type:'all-in'} — No-Limit rules: min bet = big blind, min raise = previous bet/raise increment (an all-in short raise does not reopen betting for players who already acted), action order (preflop starts left of the big blind; heads-up the button is the small blind and acts first preflop, last postflop). legalMoves must return a SMALL representative set of sizes for bets/raises (e.g. min, ½ pot, ¾ pot, pot, all-in — deduplicated) but checkMove/applyMove must accept ANY legal amount (the UI has a slider). Side pots for unequal all-ins (needed even with equal starting stacks when the option changes stacks; test it). Showdown: hand-eval.ts with evaluate7(cards) → comparable rank + name ('Two Pair, Kings and Sevens', 'Straight to the Nine', wheel A-2-3-4-5), split pots with odd chips to the first winner left of the button. Units: 1 unit = 1 chip; humanNetUnits = final stack − starting stack; maxLossUnits = 100 (starting stack). Bots: easy = calls a lot, rarely raises, folds trash to big bets; normal = preflop hand-strength chart by position + postflop made-hand/draw strength and a quick Monte-Carlo equity estimate (≤ 150 samples using the bot rng) vs. pot odds, with occasional small bluffs. No hidden-info cheating: the equity sim must sample opponents' cards from the unseen deck, never peek. Flags: folded if the learner folded; bigPot if |net| ≥ 30; luckyLastCard if the learner won at showdown with a hand that was behind on the turn and improved on the river (compare best hands on the turn vs the eventual loser); comeback if the learner was all-in and behind on the flop/turn but won; closeFinish if decided by a kicker; perfect for winning with a straight flush or four of a kind; tags e.g. 'royal-flush', 'bluff-win' (won without showdown after betting/raising). Content: order 130, featured true. Teach hand rankings with scenes (a lesson step per group of rankings is fine), blinds, the four betting rounds, check/call/raise/fold, showdown, position basics.

## Board notes (UI)

Oval felt table: up to 6 seats around (bots with avatar, name, stack, current bet chips in front, FOLDED/ALL-IN badges, dealer button 'D', SB/BB markers, face-down hole cards revealed at showdown). Centre: community board (5 slots; flop/turn/river animate in) and the pot (main + side pots). Learner: 2 hole cards + stack + 'Your best hand: Pair of Kings' helper. Action bar: Fold, Check or Call (shows amount), Bet/Raise with a slider (keyboard accessible range input with labelled min/max) + quick buttons Min, ½ Pot, Pot, All-in, and a confirm button (test ids holdem-fold, holdem-check, holdem-call, holdem-raise, holdem-allin, holdem-amount). Hand-rankings cheat sheet popover. At showdown, show each revealed hand's name and the winning five cards highlighted.
